# Sandblade

A mobile-first neon hill rider: race a ninja on a glowing blade from island to island across a night city, before the storm eclipses the moon.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any browser (phone or desktop). On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" for a full-screen app feel.

## Controls
- **Hold** anywhere: get heavy and dive down the hills (works in the air too)
- **Let go**: get light, launch off rises and glide
- Desktop: Space or Down arrow
- Pause: the II button (or Esc / P). The game also pauses itself if you switch apps.

Dive on the way down a hill, let go on the way up, and land smoothly on a downslope to chain combos; three in a row starts **Fever** (extra speed, double score and shards). Speed comes from riding well: a smooth landing after a dive gives you a kick, a bad landing on an uphill slows you down.

Each **island** is long and ends in a huge drop and a giant ramp that throws you across the water. The jump keeps the speed you brought to it (no free boost) and sets you down on a steep landing slope, so you carry that energy straight into a slide and whip back up. Reaching a new island also pushes the storm back. Every island has its own colours: 14 hand-made ones, then **Spectrum Run** on every 15th island, whose colours drift from blue through green and yellow to red as you ride it, then two-colour islands that blend from one colour into the other.

The storm gives you a 2 minute head start, then speeds up. Ride hands-off and it catches you a couple of minutes later; good riding stays ahead of it for much longer. When you fly so high that the ground leaves the screen, a marker on the bottom edge shows where you'll land: green for a smooth downhill landing, red for a bad one (hold to dive and change it).

Power-ups show up often, mostly Gale speed boosts and Skyward jumps. About a third sit on the sand where you ride through them; the rest float a hop above it, so jump to catch them.

## Power-ups
- **Gale**: full speed for a few seconds and pushes the storm back
- **Skyward**: launches you sky high and lets you glide
- **Magnet**: pulls nearby shards to you
- **Ward**: saves you from the storm once
- **Time Warp**: throws the storm far back and freezes it for 6 seconds
- **Prism**: shards are worth triple for 10 seconds, plus a trail of shards ahead
- **Spring**: for 8 seconds every landing bounces you back up

**My music** (on the menu, pause and game-over screens) plays your own songs while you ride.

Single file, no dependencies: HTML5 canvas and Web Audio.
