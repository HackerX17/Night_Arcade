NIGHT REEF

A reef that glows in the dark. Eat smaller fish, grow, and become the biggest light in the deep.

Swim toward your mouse, drag a finger anywhere, or use the arrow keys or a stick. Dash with a click, Space, the Dash button or A. Eat anything smaller than you; the bar at the top shows what you can eat. Bigger fish eat you, jellies sting, and puffers puff up, so dash in before they do. Eat in quick succession to fill the thin bar and start a Feast. Fill the big bar to become the apex of the reef.
