# Night Reef

A reef that glows in the dark. Eat smaller fish, grow, and become the biggest light in the deep.

- You start as a tiny glowing fish. **Swim toward your mouse**; on a phone, **drag anywhere** (your fish swims the way you drag, faster the further you drag). The arrow keys or WASD work too, and on a controller the stick swims (push it further to swim faster).
- **Dash** for a quick burst: click (left or right button), Space or Shift, the **Dash** button on a phone (or tap with a second finger), or A or a bumper on a controller. It recharges in a second and a half.
- **Eat anything clearly smaller than you.** The bar at the top shows every creature on the reef: the bright ones you can eat now, the ones with a red glow can eat you. Your growth bar fills as you eat; each of the six sizes unlocks bigger prey, and the camera pulls back as you grow.
- **Bigger hunters eat you.** Ribbon eels, the lurejaw (it lunges at whatever comes near its lure) and the Gloomjaw shark hunt anything smaller. They tire after a few seconds of chasing; a red marker at the edge of the screen shows one coming from off screen. Being eaten costs a life; you come back blinking for a moment.
- **Jellies sting** and **urchins** on the floor sting too (later dives), unless you're big enough to eat the jelly. A sting slows you and costs a little growth, never a size.
- **Puffbulbs puff up** when something that could eat them comes close, and a puffed-up puffer stings. Dash in before it swells.
- Smaller fish flee, but they tire: keep after them. Schools of sparkfry and neon darts are quick meals; eating in quick succession builds a **combo** (more points), and a long streak fills the thin bar and starts a **Feast**: seven seconds of double growth and points, a little extra speed, and food drawn into your mouth.
- **Pearls** drift by now and then: Bubble shield (blocks one bite or sting), Vortex (pulls food into your mouth), Ink cloud (hunters lose sight of you), Surge (faster, with quick dashes) and, rarely, an extra life.
- Fill the bar at size six to become **the apex of the reef** and finish the dive. Stars: one for finishing, one for losing no lives, one for beating the time. Each of the 14 dives has its own colour scheme (the same night schemes as the other games) and gets a little harder; Dives on the main menu replays any you've reached.
- Stars unlock four more fish to play as, on the main menu.

The note button (and Music & sound on the pause screen) picks the music: the game's own deep dub soundtrack, your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects. Escape or P pauses; the pause screen has Restart dive and Main menu.

**Original** draws everything on a 2D canvas: dark-bodied fish with glowing outlines and markings, coral, kelp with light pulsing up it, plankton that lights up where you swim through it, moonlight shafts and the moon over the waterline. **3D** (the Graphics switch on the main menu or the pause screen) models every creature in real time (fins that ripple, tails that beat, a jelly's pulsing bell, a puffer's spines, the lurejaw's jaw and lure) and lights them like paint under a black light, on a reef that rolls away into the dark. Both play exactly the same. Progress stays on your device.
