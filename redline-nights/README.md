# Redline Nights

An original neon street racer: free-roam a night city, race, run from the police, and climb the **Redline Ten**.

## The city
A 12 × 12 grid of neon blocks with four-lane roads, glowing curbs, wet asphalt, street lamps, plazas you can cut across, rooftop billboards for made-up brands, and traffic that drives its lanes, turns at crossings and pulls around anything stopped in its way.

## Driving
- Arcade handling with real slides: tap the **handbrake** going into a corner to throw the car sideways, then steer through it. Hard turns at high speed start to slide on their own.
- **Nitro** for bursts of speed. Drifting and driving flat out refill it.
- **Drift points** for every slide; drifts strung together within 2.5 s build a multiplier up to ×4. Hitting a wall mid-drift loses the drift.
- Eight original cars, from the Kestrel GT you start with to the Redline Zero. Upgrade engine, nitro, tyres and body in the **Garage**, and pick paint and underglow colours.

## Races
Glowing pillars around the city are events. Drive into one and race:
- **Sprint**: point to point against three rivals.
- **Circuit**: laps around a loop.
- **Speed Trap**: hit the speed cameras; beat the target total.
- **Drift**: score drift points before time runs out.
- **Checkpoint**: beat the clock to the finish.

Wins pay cash. **Quick race** on the title screen runs any event any time (prizes only count in your career).

## The police
- Speed past a patrol car, drift in front of one or hit one, and a pursuit starts.
- **Heat** rises the longer it goes and the more you wreck: more units, faster cars, **roadblocks** from heat 2 (with **spike strips** from heat 3), heavy units and a **helicopter** from heat 4.
- Ram police cars to disable them. Drive through an orange ring at speed to drop a **crash-spot** sign tower onto the road behind you.
- Stop with a police car on you and you're **busted**: you lose that pursuit's bounty and pay a fine.
- Break line of sight and get some distance to fill the **evade** meter, then stay hidden through the **cooldown**. The **hide spots** (blue canopies in some plazas) cool you off three times as fast. Escaping banks your bounty, plus some cash.

## The Redline Ten
Ten racers, each needing a number of event wins and a pursuit bounty before they'll race you one on one. Beat them to win their cars. Beat #1, then survive one last run from the whole city.

## Controls
- **Phone**: drag left/right on the left half of the screen to steer (or switch to **Tilt** in Settings and lean the phone like a wheel; **Center** recalibrates). Brake, nitro and drift buttons on the right; auto-accelerate is on by default (Settings).
- **Keyboard**: arrows or WASD to drive, Space handbrake, Shift nitro, Enter to start an event, C camera, M map, R back on the road, Esc pause.
- **Gamepad**: left stick steers, RT gas, LT brake, A handbrake, B nitro, Start pause.

Settings also choose MPH or KM/H, the camera (chase, far, hood), graphics quality (auto lowers it if the frame rate drops) and vibration. Your career saves on your device.

The note button picks the music: the game's own outrun soundtrack (battle techno in pursuits), your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects.

Built with three.js (vendored in `vendor/`, MIT licence). Open `index.html` from a web server (it uses JavaScript modules), or play it on the Night Arcade site.
