# Chopper Cave

A neon cave-flying game: steer a glowing helicopter through a tunnel of light for as long as you can.

- **Hold** (tap and hold, mouse, Space or Up arrow) to climb; **let go** to drop.
- Controls react the moment you touch: a touch catches the helicopter on the spot, even the quickest tap gives a short burst of lift, and the engine keeps running while any finger is down (so swapping thumbs doesn't cut it).
- Dodge the cave walls and the pillars. The cave starts roomy, narrows and speeds up the further you get, but never closes below a comfortable gap.
- Every 250 m the cave changes colour scheme, through the same neon schemes as the other Night Arcade games.
- **Power-ups** float in the open part of the cave. Fly through one to take it:
  - **Shield** (cyan): takes one crash. It bursts, you bounce back into the gap and blink for a moment.
  - **Wide Cave** (lime): for 8 seconds the walls pull back and the pillars shrink, then it all eases back.
  - **Slow-Mo** (violet): the cave slows down for 6 seconds.
  - **Portal** (magenta and gold): a jump to **Hyperspace**, a synthwave bonus world with a giant striped sun and a weaving rainbow laser tunnel. Grab orbs (+3 m) and fly through rings (+20 m), and dodge the laser gates. It gets faster and tighter the longer you last. Crashing there doesn't end your run: you drop back into the cave with a moment's shield, and the bonus is added to your distance. The second power-up of a run is always a portal, so you'll find Hyperspace early.
- Your best distance is saved on your device.

The note button (and Music & sound on the pause screen) picks the music: the game's own techno soundtrack, your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects. Opening it pauses the flight.

Open `index.html` in any browser. It plays best with the phone turned sideways.
Single file, no dependencies.
