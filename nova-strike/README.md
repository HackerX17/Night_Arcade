# Nova Strike

A neon vertical shooter: hold the line against waves of invaders and the giants that lead them.

- **Drag** anywhere to fly (your ship follows your finger's movement, so your thumb never covers it); your guns fire on their own. On a keyboard: arrows or WASD, **Shift** to fly slow and precise (it shows your hitbox).
- Only the glowing core of your ship can be hit.
- Pick-ups: **P** powers up your guns (five levels, up to a five-way spread with side cannons), **L** a laser beam, **H** homing missiles, **S** a shield that takes one hit, **B** a bomb.
- **Bombs** wipe every bullet off the screen and hurt everything on it (the bomb button, or B / Space). You start with three.
- Chains of kills build a score multiplier up to ×8; getting hit resets it.
- Every fifth wave a boss arrives, each with three phases: **Hexcore** (spirals, turrets you can shoot off, a sweeping laser), **Mantis** (fans of bullets, escorts, a cross laser) and **Nova Eye** (rings, homing orbs, a spiral storm). Beat one for a big bonus and an extra life; after the third they come round again, tougher.
- You have three lives; your high score stays on your device.

The note button (and Music & sound on the pause screen) picks the music: the game's own trance soundtrack, your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects.

Open `index.html` in any browser. Single file, no dependencies.
