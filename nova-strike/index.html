NOVASTRIKE

Hold the line against waves of neon invaders and the giants that lead them.

Drag anywhere to fly; your guns fire on their own (arrows or WASD on a keyboard, Shift to fly slow and precise). Only the glowing core of your ship can be hit. Catch P to power up your guns, L for a laser, H for homing missiles, S for a shield, B for a bomb. Bombs wipe the screen of bullets (button, or B / Space). A boss arrives every fifth wave.
