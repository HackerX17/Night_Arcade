# Prism Breaker

A neon block breaker for one thumb: aim, fire a stream of light, and break every block before it crosses the line.

- **Drag** to aim (drag below the floor to pull back like a slingshot), **let go** to fire. On a keyboard: Left/Right to aim (Shift for bigger steps), Space or Enter to fire, Down to call the balls back.
- Every ball you own fires in a stream. Each hit takes 1 off a block's number; at 0 it shatters.
- Each turn a new row slides in from the top. If a block crosses the dashed line, the game is over. Blocks one row from the line flash red.
- **+1 rings** give you another ball for the next turn (one in every row). **Stars** buy new ball colours on the main menu.
- **Lasers** zap a whole row or column every time a ball passes through them. **Prisms** split a ball into an extra one for the turn. **Bombs** blast all eight blocks around them when they break.
- Turns speed up on their own the longer they last (2×, then 3×), and the arrow button calls every ball back at once.
- Your game is saved after every turn: leave any time and pick it up with **Continue**. Your best level and stars stay on your device.

The note button (and Music & sound on the pause screen) picks the music: the game's own chill house soundtrack, your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects.

Open `index.html` in any browser. Single file, no dependencies.
