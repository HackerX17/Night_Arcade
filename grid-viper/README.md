# Grid Viper

Classic snake, neon bright. Eat the light, grow longer, and never bite your own tail.

- The snake waits on the board until you make the first move, then never stops. Steer with the **arrow keys or WASD**, **swipe** anywhere on a phone, tap the on-screen **D-pad** (it appears under the board on phones held upright), or use a **controller** (stick or D-pad).
- You can't turn straight back on yourself, and quick taps are remembered: press two turns fast and the snake takes them on the next two squares.
- **Eat the glowing dot** to grow one square. You speed up a little with every dot.
- After every fifth dot a **golden star** appears for a few seconds. It's worth five dots and makes you two squares longer. Its ring shows how long it has left.
- **Hit a wall or your own body and it's over.** Chasing your own tail is fine: the square your tail is leaving is free.
- Modes: **Classic** (walls all around), **No walls** (slide off one edge and come back on the other), and **Maze** (neon walls inside the board too, a new layout each game).
- Speeds: **Chill**, **Normal** and **Turbo**. Faster speeds score more per dot. Your best score is kept for every mode and speed, on your device.
- Skins: Viper, Magma, Ultraviolet and Ice from the start; **Gold** unlocks after 100 dots eaten in total, **Rainbow** after 250.

Escape, P or the pause button pauses; the pause screen has Restart and Main menu. The note button (and Music & sound on the pause screen) picks the music: the game's own electro house soundtrack, your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects.

Everything is drawn on a 2D canvas in one file: a glass board, a glowing tube of a snake that slides smoothly from square to square, with eyes that watch the dot, a blink and a flick of the tongue. It works offline once loaded.
