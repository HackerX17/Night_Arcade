# Glowline

A neon snake arena. Eat the light, grow longer, and make every other snake run into you.

- Your snake never stops. It **follows your mouse**. On a phone, **drag anywhere** (the snake heads the way you drag). On a controller, **point the stick** where you want to go. The arrow keys (or A and D) turn it.
- **Boost**: hold the mouse button, Space or ↑, the **Boost** button on a phone, or A, B or a trigger on a controller. Boosting doubles your speed but burns length (more the bigger you are) and leaves a trail of light behind you.
- **Eat the glowing dots** to grow. Light near your head drifts into your mouth. The longer you get, the thicker you get, and the camera pulls back so you can see more.
- **If your head touches another snake's body, you're out**, and you burst into light that anyone can eat. Your own body is safe, so you can coil around smaller snakes and trap them.
- **Make them crash into you.** Cut in front of a snake and when its head hits you, it bursts into light: that's a takedown.
- The arena is a circle with a glowing red wall. Touching the wall ends your run too.
- Twenty-two rival snakes roam the arena. Each has its own name, skin and skill: some look far ahead, some are reckless, and the big ones hunt smaller snakes by cutting them off.
- The leaderboard (top right) shows the ten longest snakes and your rank. The minimap (bottom left) shows the arena, you, and the biggest snakes.
- **Skins**: pick from sixteen neon skins on the main menu. Rainbow, Gold and Galaxy unlock when you end a run at length 1,500, 4,000 and 8,000. Type a name and it shows over your snake and on the leaderboard.
- When you crash you see your final length, your best rank, takedowns and time alive. Your best length stays on your device.

The note button (and Music & sound on the pause screen) picks the music: the game's own techno soundtrack, your own songs or music app, or none, with a separate switch for sound effects. Escape or P pauses; the pause screen has Restart and Main menu.

Everything is drawn on a 2D canvas: the snakes are built from glossy neon beads, the light glows, and the arena is a starfield with a faint grid. It runs in any modern browser and works offline once loaded.
